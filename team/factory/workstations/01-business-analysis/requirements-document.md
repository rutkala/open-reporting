# Requirements Document Component

## Produces
- Requirements Document (analytical brief with KPIs, benchmarks)

## Internal DAG
See `dag/requirements-document.yaml`

## Step Instructions

### Step 1: Research Domain
Research the business/economic domain using relevant knowledge bases.

**Actions:**
- Review relevant domain KB (e.g., public-finance, labour)
- Research authoritative sources
- Document key findings

**Output:** Domain Research Summary

---

### Step 2: Audience Identification
Determine who will use the product and their needs.

**Actions:**
- Define target audience
- Identify their analytical questions
- Determine their expertise level

**Output:** Audience Profile

---

### Step 3: Define KPIs
Define primary and secondary KPIs using SMART+FABRIC framework.

**Actions:**
- Define Specific KPIs
- Ensure Measurable
- Verify Achievable
- Confirm Relevant
- Set Time-bound reference periods
- Apply FABRIC criteria

**Output:** KPI List

---

### Step 4: Define Benchmarks
Define reference values for each KPI.

**Actions:**
- Research EU27 averages
- Research V4 averages
- Define prior year benchmarks
- Define policy targets

**Output:** Benchmark Definitions

---

### Step 5: Create Brief
Compile the analytical brief.

**Actions:**
- Write executive summary
- Document KPIs with definitions
- Include benchmarks
- Define analytical angles

**Output:** Draft Requirements Document

---

### Step 6: Review
Review the document for completeness.

**Actions:**
- Verify all KPIs are SMART
- Verify benchmarks are documented
- Check Polish context
- Verify aggregation correctness

**Output:** Review Comments

---

### Step 7: Finalize
Finalize the document.

**Actions:**
- Incorporate review feedback
- Final Polish
- Mark as ready

**Output:** Final Requirements Document

---

## Input Requirements
- Product request (what to build)
- Target audience
- Success criteria

## Output
- Requirements Document
