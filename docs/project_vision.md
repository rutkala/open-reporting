# Project: Open Reporting
## The idea
Public data exists on everything — economy, politics, health, society, sport, environment, demographics, crime, education and more. But it's scattered, hard to access, poorly visualized, and rarely told as a story.

Open Reporting collects it, processes it, and turns it into products people actually want to read, watch, and use.

Started as an idea ~8 years ago. Now materializing with AI-assisted engineering. Long-term project — agile approach, start simple, grow organically.

---

## Four product lines
**1. Analytical Portal** — Interactive dashboards organized by domain. portal.open-reporting.dev

**2. Content Portal / Blog** — Data-driven articles and reports organized by domain, with a main feed across all topics. www.open-reporting.dev

**3. Mobile App** — Same data pipeline, mobile-first interface. Future phase.

**4. Social Media** — High-frequency short-form content across LinkedIn, X, Instagram. No domain grouping — publish often across all topics.

---

## Audience
* **General public** — Polish citizens curious about their country
* **Analysts & researchers** — journalists, academics, think tanks
* **Professionals** — consultants, financial analysts, policy makers
* **International** — EU researchers, investors, expats studying Poland and CEE

---

## Language strategy
Polish-first. English available for key content and the portal. Write once, publish in both.

---

## Business model (target)
* **Free** — standard dashboards, articles, basic data access
* **Subscription** — premium analysis, advanced dashboards, data exports, API access
* **Sponsorship / partnerships** — data providers, institutions
* **Licensing** — processed datasets for organizations
