## Key Paragraph Text

**Highlights:**
- Euro area government deficit/GDP ratio decreased from -3.0% in 2024 to -2.9% in 2025
- EU ratio remained unchanged at -3.1%
- In 2025, all Member States except Cyprus (+3.4%), Denmark (+2.9%), Ireland (+1.8%), Greece (+1.7%) and Portugal (+0.7%) reported a deficit

**Introduction:**
The Stability and Growth Pact (SGP) requires member states to maintain government deficit not exceeding 3% of GDP, and debt not exceeding 60% of GDP.

**Government Revenue and Expenditure:**
In 2025, government expenditure in the euro area was equivalent to 49.8% of GDP and government revenue to 46.9% of GDP.
