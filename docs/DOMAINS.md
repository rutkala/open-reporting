# Open Reporting — Domain Taxonomy

Domains define the thematic areas we cover. Each domain maps to a set of data sources, KPIs, and dashboard topics.

## Active Domains

### Finance & Capital Markets
- GPW (Warsaw Stock Exchange) — equity prices, indices, volume
- NBP (National Bank of Poland) — exchange rates, interest rates, money supply
- KNF — financial sector data

### Public Finance & Budget
- OpenBudget (`openbudget.gov.pl`) — central government budget execution
- Ministry of Finance — tax revenues, debt, expenditure by function
- Local government budgets (gminas, powiats, voivodeships)

### Demographics & Society
- GUS BDL — population, births, deaths, migration
- Eurostat — EU comparisons, cohorts
- ZUS — social insurance, pensions

### Labour Market
- GUS BDL — employment, unemployment by region and sector
- BAEL — Labour Force Survey
- Wages by sector and voivodeship

### Health
- GUS — life expectancy, mortality causes
- NFZ (National Health Fund) — healthcare utilisation, waiting lists
- NIZP-PZH — epidemiology

### Education
- MEN — school data, graduates, outcomes
- GUS — education attainment by age/region
- Eurostat — EU comparisons

### Infrastructure & Environment
- GDDKiA — road network
- GUS — energy consumption, CO2 emissions
- KOBIZE — greenhouse gas inventory

### Culture & Quality of Life
- GUS — cultural institutions, access to services
- CSI (Culture Statistics Index)

---

## Domain Prioritisation

Prioritise domains where:
1. Data is freely available via API (no scraping)
2. High public interest / search demand
3. Data is not already well-visualised by others
4. Time-series data available (trends are more interesting than snapshots)

Current priority order is managed in Linear (project: Open Reporting).
