# Open Reporting — Product Vision

**v0.1 · March 2026 · Long-term personal project**

---

## Mission

A one-person data media company that transforms public data into
accessible, beautiful, and useful products — covering every aspect
of public life that data can describe.

Built incrementally, designed to last.

---

## The idea

Public data exists on everything — economy, politics, health,
society, sport, environment, demographics, crime, education and
more. But it's scattered, hard to access, poorly visualized, and
rarely told as a story.

Open Reporting collects it, processes it, and turns it into
products people actually want to read, watch, and use.

Started as an idea ~8 years ago. Now materializing with
AI-assisted engineering. This is a long-term project —
agile approach, start simple, grow organically.

---

## Topic domains (target)

The portal and blog are organized by topic. Any domain where
public data exists is in scope.

**Economics & Finance**
Budget, public finances, taxes, debt, inflation, interest rates,
banking, investment, stock markets

**Politics & Government**
Elections, voting patterns, parliament, government spending,
public procurement, transparency

**Society & Demographics**
Population, migration, age structure, households, inequality,
poverty, social transfers

**Labour Market**
Employment, unemployment, wages, sectors, regional disparities,
job market trends

**Health**
Healthcare spending, mortality, disease statistics, hospital
data, pharmaceutical market

**Education**
Schools, universities, literacy, outcomes, spending, regional
differences

**Environment**
Emissions, air quality, energy consumption, renewable energy,
climate indicators

**Infrastructure & Transport**
Roads, railways, public transport, investment, accidents

**Sport**
Results, statistics, federation data, attendance, funding

**Crime & Justice**
Crime statistics, court data, prison population, police data

**Regional / Local**
All of the above broken down by voivodship, county, municipality —
Poland's unique strength

## Four product lines

### 1. Analytical Portal
Interactive dashboards, charts, and data explorer.
Organized by topic. Progressive depth:
raw data → clean charts → interactive dashboards → data science.
For analysts, researchers, power users.
**portal.open-reporting.dev**

### 2. Content Portal / Blog
Articles, analyses, reports, presentations — human-written
stories built from the data. Free tier plus subscription/paywall
for premium content. For general audience.
**www.open-reporting.dev**

### 3. Mobile App(s)
Data products on smartphones. Same pipeline, different interface.
Push notifications for data updates. Future phase.

### 4. Social Media / Video
Daily short-form on LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok.
Long-form video on YouTube. Turns data into shareable stories.
Drives audience back to portal and blog.

## Audience

- **General public** — Polish citizens curious about their country
- **Analysts & researchers** — journalists, academics, think tanks
- **Professionals** — consultants, financial analysts, policy makers
- **International** — EU researchers, investors, expats, anyone
  studying Poland and CEE

---

## Language strategy

**Polish-first** — this is Polish public data, Polish audience is
primary. English available for key content and the analytical
portal. AI-assisted translation means write once, publish in both.

---

## Business model (target)

- **Free** — standard dashboards, articles, basic data access
- **Subscription** — premium analysis, advanced dashboards,
  data exports, API access, early content
- **Sponsorship / partnerships** — data providers, institutions
- **Licensing** — processed datasets for organizations

*Completely separate from B2B Power BI / Fabric consulting.*

---

## Technology principles

- Collect once, distribute everywhere
- Lean stack — scales up only when needed
- AI-assisted engineering — Claude Code handles implementation,
  Radek handles product and editorial decisions
- Open where possible, proprietary where it creates value

## Phases

**Now — MVP**
3 dashboards live (state budget, regional budgets, GPW market).
2 Polish articles. Basic data pipeline. VPS running.

**Phase 2 — Foundation**
More data sources. More articles. SEO. LinkedIn presence.
Automated daily data updates. English content.

**Phase 3 — Monetization**
Subscription system. Newsletter. Social media workflow.

**Phase 4 — Scale**
Mobile app. Video content. Data science analyses.
API product. Team possible.
