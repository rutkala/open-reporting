# Reference Library Index

Core academic sources used across the research knowledge base.

## Microeconomics

| Key | Author(s) | Title | Year | Notes |
|-----|-----------|-------|------|-------|
| MAS-COLELL | Mas-Colell, Whinston, Green | Microeconomic Theory | 1995 | Graduate-level reference. Consumer and producer theory, GE. |
| VARIAN-MICRO | Varian, H. | Intermediate Microeconomics | 2014 | Accessible intermediate text. |
| PINDYCK | Pindyck, Rubinfeld | Microeconomics | 2018 | Applied focus. Good on industrial organisation. |

## Macroeconomics

| Key | Author(s) | Title | Year | Notes |
|-----|-----------|-------|------|-------|
| MANKIW-MACRO | Mankiw, N.G. | Macroeconomics | 2019 | Core intermediate macro. IS-LM, AS-AD, growth. |
| BLANCHARD | Blanchard, O. | Macroeconomics | 2021 | Strong on open economy and policy. |
| ROMER-ADVANCED | Romer, D. | Advanced Macroeconomics | 2019 | Graduate-level. Growth theory, RBC, NK models. |
| ACEMOGLU | Acemoglu, D. | Introduction to Modern Economic Growth | 2009 | Definitive growth theory reference. |

## Econometrics

| Key | Author(s) | Title | Year | Notes |
|-----|-----------|-------|------|-------|
| WOOLDRIDGE | Wooldridge, J. | Introductory Econometrics | 2020 | Standard undergraduate/graduate text. OLS, IV, panel data. |
| GREENE | Greene, W. | Econometric Analysis | 2018 | Comprehensive graduate reference. |
| HAMILTON | Hamilton, J. | Time Series Analysis | 1994 | Definitive time series reference. ARIMA, VAR, cointegration. |
| STOCK-WATSON | Stock, Watson | Introduction to Econometrics | 2019 | Applied focus. Good empirical examples. |
