# P12.1 UX/UI Design Visual Map — Template Dashboard

## 1. Layout Strategy
**Pattern**: "Paper on Table" (Nordic Minimal).
**Structure**:
- **Header**: Title: "Katalog Komponentów Wizualnych" | Subtitle: "Referencyjny zestaw wzorców dla platformy Open Reporting".
- **Filter Pane**: 
    - Year (Slicer: Range)
    - Category (Slicer: List)
    - Region (Slicer: Dropdown)
- **Main Canvas**: Organized into 5 Topic Groups.

## 2. Topic Groups & Chart Mapping

### Group A: Longitudinal Trends (Time-series)
- **Chart 1**: Line Chart (Measure A vs Year) $\rightarrow$ `line()`
- **Chart 2**: Area Chart (Measure B vs Year) $\rightarrow$ `area()`
- **Chart 3**: 100% Stacked Area (Measure A, B, C vs Year) $\rightarrow$ `pct_stacked_area()`
- **Chart 4**: Line + Clustered Column (Measure A and B vs Year) $\rightarrow$ `line_clustered_column()`

### Group B: Categorical Comparison (Cross-sectional)
- **Chart 1**: Clustered Column (Measure A, B, C vs Category) $\rightarrow$ `clustered_column()`
- **Chart 2**: Stacked Bar (Measure A, B, C vs Category) $\rightarrow$ `stacked_bar()`
- **Chart 3**: 100% Stacked Column (Measure A, B, C vs Category) $\rightarrow$ `pct_stacked_column()`
- **Chart 4**: Clustered+Stacked Bar (Measure A, B, C vs Category) $\rightarrow$ `clustered_stacked_bar()`

### Group C: Geographic Distribution (Spatial)
- **Chart 1**: Choropleth Map (Measure A vs ISO3) $\rightarrow$ `choropleth_map()`
- **Chart 2**: Bubble Map (Measure A vs Lat/Lon) $\rightarrow$ `bubble_map()`

### Group D: Composition & Distribution (Structural)
- **Chart 1**: Treemap (Measure A by Hierarchy) $\rightarrow$ `treemap()`
- **Chart 2**: Funnel (Measure A by Stage) $\rightarrow$ `funnel()`
- **Chart 3**: Waterfall Contribution (Measure A by Stage) $\rightarrow$ `waterfall_contribution()`
- **Chart 4**: Histogram (Measure A distribution) $\rightarrow$ `histogram()`
- **Chart 5**: Box Plot (Measure A by Group) $\rightarrow$ `box_plot()`

### Group E: Specialized & High-Density (Advanced)
- **Chart 1**: Scatter Bubble (Measure A vs B, size Measure D) $\rightarrow$ `scatter_bubble()`
- **Chart 2**: Pie/Donut (Measure A by Category) $\rightarrow$ `pie_chart()`
- **Chart 3**: Gauge (Measure A vs Target) $\rightarrow$ `gauge()`
- **Chart 4**: Bullet (Measure A vs Target) $\rightarrow$ `bullet()`
- **Chart 5**: Basic Table (Measure A, B, C, D by Category) $\rightarrow$ `table_basic()`
- **Chart 6**: Pivot Matrix (Measure A by Cat and Period) $\rightarrow$ `table_matrix()`
- **Chart 7**: Data List (Measure A by Category) $\rightarrow$ `data_list()`
- **Chart 8**: Heatmap (Measure A by Cat and Period) $\rightarrow$ `heatmap_matrix()`
- **Chart 9**: Ribbon Chart (Measure A by Year and Category) $\rightarrow$ `ribbon()`
- **Chart 10**: Candlestick (OHLC for Measure A) $\rightarrow$ `candlestick()`

## 3. Interaction Logic
- **Global Filter**: `dim_year` $\rightarrow$ updates all charts in all groups.
- **Cross-Filter**: Selecting a Category in "Categorical Comparison" highlights the corresponding region in "Geographic Distribution".
- **Drill-through**: Clicking a category in the Treemap filters the laout to that specific category.
