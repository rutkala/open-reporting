# European Qualifications Framework (EQF) — 8 Levels

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Qualifications_Framework (Wikipedia, as the official europass.europa.eu URL returned 404)
**Retrieved:** 2026-04-19

## General definitions

- **Knowledge** — "In the context of EQF, knowledge is described as theoretical and/or factual."
- **Skills** — "In the context of EQF, skills are described as cognitive (involving the use of logical, intuitive and creative thinking) and practical (involving manual dexterity and the use of methods, materials, tools and instruments)."
- **Responsibility and Autonomy** — "In the context of the EQF responsibility and autonomy is described as the ability of the learner to apply knowledge and skills autonomously and with responsibility."

## Knowledge descriptor — by level

| Level | Knowledge descriptor |
|---|---|
| 1 | Basic general knowledge |
| 2 | Basic factual knowledge of a field of work or study |
| 3 | Knowledge of facts, principles, processes and general concepts, in a field of work or study |
| 4 | Factual and theoretical knowledge in broad contexts within a field of work or study |
| 5 | Comprehensive, specialised, factual and theoretical knowledge within a field of work or study and an awareness of the boundaries of that knowledge |
| 6 | Advanced knowledge of a field of work or study, involving a critical understanding of theories and principles |
| 7 | Highly specialised knowledge, some of which is at the forefront of knowledge in a field of work or study, as the basis for original thinking and/or research; Critical awareness of knowledge issues in a field and at the interface between different fields |
| 8 | Knowledge at the most advanced frontier of a field of work or study and at the interface between fields |

## Progression pattern

- Levels 1–2: basic, factual
- Levels 3–4: facts + principles + theories (broad contexts)
- Levels 5–6: comprehensive / specialised, critical understanding
- Levels 7–8: frontier, original thinking, cross-field synthesis

The EQF positions knowledge along a depth axis independent of skills and of the
autonomy/responsibility with which it is applied.
