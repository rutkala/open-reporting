# O*NET — Knowledge (Content Model)

**Source:** https://www.onetcenter.org/content.html
**Retrieved:** 2026-04-19

## Definition (verbatim)

> "Organized sets of principles and facts applying in general domains"

## Nine major knowledge categories

1. Business and Management — strategic planning, resource allocation, organizational leadership
2. Manufacturing and Production — production processes, quality control, goods distribution
3. Engineering and Technology — design, development, application of technology systems
4. Mathematics and Science — history, theories, applications across physical and biological disciplines
5. Health Services — diagnosis, treatment, prevention of disease and mental health
6. Education and Training — curriculum design, instruction, training measurement
7. Arts and Humanities — human thought, language, artistic expression
8. Law and Public Safety — regulations, security procedures, legal frameworks
9. Communications — media production, information delivery techniques

## Distinction from other Content Model domains

- **Knowledge** — acquired factual understanding
- **Skills** — procedures and applied techniques built on knowledge
- **Abilities** — enduring personal attributes influencing performance
- **Work Activities** — what workers actually do on the job

## Comparison to ESCO

O*NET and ESCO both separate knowledge from skills. O*NET's "organized sets of
principles and facts" maps closely to ESCO's "body of facts, principles, theories
and practices". Both treat knowledge as the foundational content; the difference
is in applied use.
