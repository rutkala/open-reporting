# DIKW Hierarchy — Data, Information, Knowledge, Wisdom

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DIKW_pyramid
**Retrieved:** 2026-04-19
**Citations:**
- Ackoff, Russell L. 1989. "From Data to Wisdom." *Journal of Applied Systems Analysis* 16: 3–9.
- Rowley, Jennifer. 2007. "The wisdom hierarchy: representations of the DIKW hierarchy." *Journal of Information Science* 33(2): 163–180.

## The four levels

| Level | Definition | Question answered |
|---|---|---|
| **Data** | Discrete, objective facts or observations — unorganised and unprocessed; symbols/signs representing stimuli (Rowley 2007). Lacks meaning due to insufficient context. | — |
| **Information** | Data that has gained relevance and context — "information is inferred from data" (Rowley). Applicable to decisions and actions. | Who / what / where / when |
| **Knowledge** | Processed information applied in practice. Ackoff (1989): "application of data and information." | How |
| **Wisdom** | Evaluated understanding — judgement about the right application of knowledge; ethical and consequential reasoning. | Why |

## The transformation chain

- **Data → Information** — contextualisation (adding meaning, relevance)
- **Information → Knowledge** — synthesis + practical application
- **Knowledge → Wisdom** — reflective evaluation of consequences and ethical
  implications

Lower levels serve as material for higher ones.

## Critiques

- **Capurro**: the logical hierarchy is a "fairytale"; each level is not a clean
  derivation of the one below.
- **Weinberger**: data and information exist in computers; knowledge and wisdom
  are distinctly human — the simple linear model oversimplifies.
- **Frické**: the model's philosophical foundations are "dated and unsatisfactory".

## Relevance to the skills framework

- Our `knowledge/` folder sits at the Knowledge level — processed information
  about a field, organised for application.
- Raw sources in `knowledge/raw/` may themselves straddle Data and Information
  levels (raw text, tables, references) — the `summary.md` is the synthesis
  that elevates them into Knowledge.
- Wisdom sits above this — it is what emerges when the skill is actually used
  in context by a competent actor. We do not attempt to codify wisdom directly;
  we aim to give the model enough knowledge + crystallised experience to
  support wise application in context.
